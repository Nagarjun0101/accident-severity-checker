Road Accident Severity Checker

Helps a road-safety analyst decide which conditions to investigate first

How to read this: pick the conditions. The tool shows how likely an accident under those conditions is to be severe (Severity 3 or 4, meaning a bigger impact on traffic). It supports prioritising investigations. It does not predict or prevent individual accidents.
